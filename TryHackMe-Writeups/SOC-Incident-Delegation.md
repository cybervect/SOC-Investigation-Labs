# Security Operations: Incident Escalation & Role Assignment

**Scenario:** Acting as a CISO for a multinational company, I was tasked with coordinating the response to multiple simultaneous security incidents and infrastructure failures. 

## Objective
To demonstrate an understanding of enterprise security team structures, proper alert escalation paths, and incident response delegation across specialized security domains.

## Incident Delegation & Routing

To ensure rapid containment and compliance, I triaged and routed seven distinct scenarios to the appropriate security units:

*   **Initial Alert Triage:** Routed a SIEM alert regarding a firewall brute-force attack to the **SOC Analyst** for initial investigation.
*   **Deep Malware Analysis:** Assigned the execution of a phishing payload by an HR manager to the **Malware Analyst** for reverse engineering and payload extraction.
*   **Active Ransomware Containment:** Escalated an active ransomware outbreak in a regional office directly to the **Incident Response (IR)** team for immediate network isolation and remediation.
*   **Threat Profiling:** Directed the analysis of the FIN7 advanced persistent threat (APT) group's tactics to the **Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)** analyst.
*   **Security Infrastructure:** Assigned a critical SIEM storage failure outage to the **SOC Engineer** to restore logging capabilities.
*   **Compliance & Auditing:** Routed the PCI DSS compliance audit for credit card servers to the **GRC Auditor**.
*   **Vulnerability Assessment:** Assigned the pre-deployment security check of a new web application version to the **Penetration Tester**.

## Conclusion
Effective security operations require strict adherence to escalation matrixes. By correctly routing alerts—separating infrastructure engineering tasks from active threat containment and deep forensic analysis—the SOC avoids bottlenecks and ensures incidents are handled by the correct subject matter experts.
